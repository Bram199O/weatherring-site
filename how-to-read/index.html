Example — not your city.

  • Temperature · Lincoln

    Cool hours sit toward blue/violet. Warm hours sit toward orange/red.

  • Night · Eureka

    Darker arc is night. It lifts at sunrise and returns at sunset.

  • Sky · Eureka

    Inner glow: yellow = clearer, white/grey = cloud or overcast.

  • Rain · São Paulo

    Streaks on that hour. Longer = more rain. Gaps = patchy.

  • Snow · Ushuaia

    Soft white ticks — snow or ice, not rain.

  • Thunder · Yangon

    Bolts when thunder is in the forecast, even if rain is light.

  • Fog · Eureka

    Milky inner band — fog or dense mist. Thicker = heavier.
