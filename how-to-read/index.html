Guide

How to read the ring

The same example face as in the app. Tap a layer to isolate it.

Example — not your city.

Midnight to midnight, with midnight at the bottom of the ring and noon at the top. Color shows temperature, and the hand points to the current hour.

The three numbers are today’s low, the current temperature and today’s high. 00 06 12 18 are clock hours.

The small slot above the time shows the watch battery by default. Long-press the watch face, tap Customise, then tap the slot to pick battery, steps, another app or Empty.

Layer by layer

The ring
One day, midnight to midnight. Midnight (00) is at the bottom, 06 on the left, noon (12) at the top and 18 on the right. Color shows temperature, and the hand points to the current hour.
Now
From midnight to the hand is time that has already passed today. The inner and outer lines are the same mark.
Temperature
Cool hours sit toward blue and violet. Warm hours sit toward orange and red.
Night
The darker arc is night. It lifts at sunrise and returns at sunset. Set its strength under Night veil in the Look tab. With Also dim weather off, rain, snow, storms and fog stay bright at night.
Sky
The glow inside the ring is the sky: gold for sun, pale for overcast, and gold and grey stripes when it’s partly cloudy. The gold only shows by day.
Rain
Streaks on that hour. Longer streaks mean more rain, gaps mean patchy showers.
Snow
Soft white ticks mean snow or ice, not rain.
Thunder
Lightning bolts show when thunder is in the forecast, even if the rain is light.
Fog
A milky band just inside the ring means fog or dense mist. A thicker band means heavier fog.
The three numbers
Today’s low, the current temperature and today’s high. 00 06 12 18 are clock hours.

Above the time

The slot above the time

Above the time sits a complication slot. By default it shows the watch battery.

  • Green from 60%
  • Yellow from 20 to 60%
  • Red below 20%

Other complications use the clock colour. To change it, long-press the watch face, tap Customise, then tap the small slot above the time and pick battery, steps, another app or Empty.

Optional

Next days

Turn on Next days in the Look tab to make the bottom half of the face a forecast for tomorrow, or for the next two days. It’s off by default.

Today then fills the top half, from 00 on the left through 12 at the top to 24 on the right. Tomorrow starts on the right, unless you turn on Tomorrow on the left. A divider marks where each day starts.

The two letters are the weekday, so FR is Friday. They follow your phone’s language. With Curved min and max on, the numbers beside them are that day’s low and high.

Each day has its own colors, rain, snow, fog, hour lines and night shading, so you read it the same way as today.
