New in 1.1

New in WeatherRing 1.1

Three new things on the face and in the phone app, plus a round of fixes.

Next days

Turn on Next days in the Look tab and today moves to the top half of the ring. The bottom half shows tomorrow, or the next two days, each with its own temperatures and weather. Two letters mark the weekday, with that day’s low and high beside them. It’s off by default.

A slot above the time

There’s now a complication slot above the time. It shows the watch battery by default, in green from 60%, yellow from 20 to 60% and red below 20%. To show something else, long-press the face, tap Customise and pick battery, steps, another app or Empty.

Refresh every 1, 2, 3 or 6 hours

On the Watch tab you choose how often the watch gets new weather. Every hour is the default, and a longer interval saves battery. If Follow your location is on, your location updates at the same time.

Also in 1.1

  • The date and weekday letters on the watch follow your phone’s language. In Dutch the date reads DO 8 OKT, in German DO 8. OKT. The app’s own screens are still in English.
  • Larger text in the phone app.
  • The night veil can leave rain, snow, storms and fog bright at night. Turn off Also dim weather under Night veil.
  • Fixes: the watch app no longer crashes when it falls back to the watch’s own GPS, weather refresh works again on Android 10 and 11 phones, and the watch no longer shows yesterday’s weather after midnight.

Location

Location is optional

Search any city, or tap "Use my location" for a quick one-time check. Follow your location is a separate switch, off by default. It needs "Allow all the time" and updates at the same interval as Refresh every. Saved cities never run background GPS.

FAQ

Questions

Is it one purchase?

Yes. One purchase covers the phone companion and the Wear OS watch app. You still install twice: first on the phone, then the same app on the watch.

Do I need location turned on?

No. You can search for any city manually. Tapping "Use my location" only checks your position once and won't turn on background tracking. Follow your location is a separate switch and is off by default.

How often does the weather update?

Every hour by default. Under Refresh every on the Watch tab you can pick 1, 2, 3 or 6 hours. A longer interval saves battery. If the phone is out of reach, the watch fetches the weather itself.

Can I see tomorrow's weather?

Yes. Turn on Next days in the Look tab. Today moves to the top half of the ring and the bottom half shows tomorrow, or the next two days. It's off by default.

What's the small icon above the time?

That's the complication slot. It shows the watch battery by default: green from 60%, yellow from 20 to 60%, red below 20%. To show something else, long-press the watch face, tap Customise, then tap the slot and pick battery, steps, another app or Empty.

Can I customize the Always-On Display?

Yes. In the Look tab, open the Always-on section and choose what stays visible when the watch screen dims: temperature ring, sky glow, precipitation, time, date, center temperatures and city name. These settings don't change the face when the watch is awake.

Is the app in my language?

The date and the weekday letters on the watch follow your phone's language. The app's own screens are in English.

Which watches does it work on?

Requires Wear OS 6 or newer. Not compatible with Wear OS 5 or older. It also needs a round display.

Does it work on rectangular Wear OS or Apple Watch?

No. WeatherRing is for round Wear OS 6+ watches only. Not rectangular Wear OS. Not Apple Watch.

Do I need an account? Are there ads?

No account. No ads. We do not sell data. Settings and the day’s forecast stay on the phone and watch.
