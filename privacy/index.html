Policy

Privacy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

WeatherRing is a 24-hour weather watch face and phone companion. One Google Play purchase covers the phone app and the Wear OS watch app.

What we collect

  • Location, on the device (optional). If you choose Current location, the phone (and, if needed, the watch) reads GPS so we can fetch weather for that place. A chosen city is stored as a name and coordinates. You can search a city instead and never grant location.
  • Weather for that place. The phone requests a forecast over HTTPS. The watch does the same only if the phone data is stale.
  • Appearance and the current day’s ring. Settings, the 24-hour forecast cache, and the chosen place stay on the device and sync between the paired phone and watch over the Wear OS Data Layer.

We do not create accounts. We do not show ads. We do not sell data.

Where data goes

WeatherRing sends the place you chose over HTTPS:

  • Forecast — coordinates to weather.weatherring.net (self-hosted Open-Meteo).
  • City search — the typed city name to WeatherAPI.com (api.weatherapi.com).
  • Live GPS place name — a current device GPS fix on the phone only to BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net). Not used for a saved city. The watch does not call BigDataCloud.

Wear Data Layer traffic stays on the paired phone and watch.

Location, settings, and the day’s cache live in app storage. Uninstalling WeatherRing removes them. We do not keep a server-side profile.

Permissions

  • Approximate / precise location — optional.
    • Use my location (app on screen) may use precise location for a one-shot fix. It does not turn on hourly follow and does not ask for “Allow all the time.”
    • Update place about once an hour is off until you turn it on under Location. Then WeatherRing reads approximate location about once an hour so the weather on the watch can stay current when you travel. That needs “Allow all the time.” Turn the switch off anytime; the last coordinates stay and weather still refreshes for that place.
    • A chosen city never uses hourly background location. You can search a city instead and never grant location.
  • Internet — HTTPS to weather.weatherring.net (forecast), WeatherAPI.com (city search), and BigDataCloud (live GPS place name on the phone).
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth — Wear OS Data Layer with the paired watch.

Children

WeatherRing is not directed at children.
